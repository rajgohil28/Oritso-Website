Case Study

Case Study 1

Post Office Savings Bank (India Post)

Oritso designed and deployed a centralized Cheque Truncation System (CTS) infrastructure for the Post Office Savings Bank, enabling seamless cheque processing across one of the largest and most distributed banking networks in India. The solution was built to accommodate operational realities, including limited connectivity, manual workflows, and decentralized branch operations.

By introducing a centrally controlled architecture with branch-level flexibility, Oritso ensured uninterrupted clearing operations even amid branch-level disruptions. The platform has consistently supported regulatory compliance, scaled with increasing transaction volumes, and enabled POSB to transition smoothly to advanced frameworks such as the Continuous Cheque Truncation System (CCTS).
