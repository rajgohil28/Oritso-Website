Kiosk Banking

One Kiosk. Complete Self-Service Banking.

What is Kiosk Banking?

ORITSO's Kiosk Banking brings together self-service banking transactions, account services, printing services, and digital banking access in a single branch or self-service unit. Customers can check balances, print passbooks and mini-statements, request cheque books, and complete other everyday banking tasks without depending on a teller.

Why Does It Matter?

Customers increasingly expect fast, self-directed access to routine banking services. Kiosk Banking consolidates multiple self-service capabilities into a single access point, reducing branch congestion and giving institutions a flexible, low-cost way to extend service hours and reach.

Key Features & Benefits

Self-Service Banking Transactions
Account Services
Printing Services
Digital Banking Access
Core Banking Integration
Flexible Deployment

Best Suited For

Banks and financial institutions looking to consolidate self-service banking, account services, and printing capabilities into a single kiosk, reducing branch workload and improving customer convenience.
