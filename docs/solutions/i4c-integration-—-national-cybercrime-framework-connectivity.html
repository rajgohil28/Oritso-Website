I4C Integration: National Cybercrime Framework Connectivity

I4C Integration: National Cybercrime Framework Connectivity

From Detection to Enforcement. Seamlessly Connected.

What is I4C Integration?

ORITSO's I4C Integration connects institutions directly to the Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C) and the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS). This enables real-time cross-verification against the National Suspect Registry and coordinated action on suspected fraud — without manual back-and-forth.

Why Does It Matter?

Fraud detection alone isn't enough if it can't translate into enforcement. This integration closes the gap between identifying a threat and acting on it within the national cybercrime framework, helping institutions freeze mule accounts, halt suspicious transactions, and remain aligned with PMLA guidelines without delays from manual coordination.

Key Features & Benefits

Real-Time I4C & CFCFRMS Integration

Immediate Transaction Intervention

National Suspect Registry Cross-Verification

PMLA Compliance Alignment

Automatic Mule Account Flagging

Best Suited For:

Banks and NBFCs handling retail digital payments currently rely on manual coordination with law enforcement and the cybercrime helpline (1930) for fund-freeze requests. Also suited for institutions needing to demonstrate NSR cross-verification for RBI or PMLA compliance audits, and payment aggregators or PPIs exposed to mule-account-driven fraud at the onboarding or transaction stage.
